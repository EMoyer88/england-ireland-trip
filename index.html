DAY 4Thu, Oct 15

Belfast, for the Titanic

Dublin → Belfast, ~2 hrs via the M1

9:00 AM
Depart Dublin
Mostly motorway, the easiest drive of the trip. Crosses into Northern Ireland — no border stop, but bring a passport.
~11:30 AM
Titanic Belfast
Built on the actual shipyard slipways where the ship was laid down and launched. Budget 2.5–3 hours.
Evening
Cathedral Quarter
Dinner, then The Duke of York or The Dirty Onion for live music. Overnight in Belfast.

Why this is the day the trip is built around

Titanic was designed, built, and launched at the Harland & Wolff yard here — the museum sits directly on that slipway, not a replica site elsewhere. It's rated among the best museums in Ireland or the UK, and the scale of the thing is hard to get from photos.

Book the SS Nomadic add-on if there's time — it's the last surviving White Star Line vessel, and it's included in the ticket.

Logistics
Currency switches to GBP once you cross into Northern Ireland. No formal border checkpoint, but carry a passport. Book Titanic Belfast tickets ahead for a fixed entry slot — it does sell out on busy days.
